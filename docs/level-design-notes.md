# Mario level study → original Mass Quest layouts

Reviewed during the five-act iteration at the user's request. These references guide pacing and teaching; none of their maps, sprites, music, or exact obstacle sequences are included in this game.

| Reference studied | Observation from the reference | Our adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Super Mario Bros. opening / World 1-1 | Nintendo's creators explain how encounters teach jumping, stomping, and why a mushroom is beneficial through play. | Festival Run places growth on safe ground before hazards, then an optional star above an approachable platform. A long safe runway separates teaching from the first gap. |
| Super Mario Bros. World 1-3 | The documented course alternates islands of different heights, collectible rewards, and moving lifts. | Papikondalu Trail alternates river banks, stepping ledges, and short moving crossings; every harder crossing has a clear landing and recovery bank. |
| Super Mario World: Donut Plains 1 | The documented level introduces a new power and offers optional high rewards and alternate exploration. | Optional upper coin/pickup routes reward exploration without requiring a power for the main route. Studio Showdown combines familiar platform movement and set obstacles instead of adding new controls. |

The specific Mass Quest placements and decisions above are our design inferences from those references. Our actors share base jump physics; jumps are sized for that controller, not Mario's. Coins indicate useful routes, checkpoints follow difficult sections, and the fortress remains the climax.
